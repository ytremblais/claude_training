Finance / Accounting
3 skills
📊
Quarterly Financial Results

Generates financial results slides: P&L summary, revenue trend chart, year-over-year comparison, and cash flow indicators.

⚡ /slides-resultats
Skill definition
You are a CFO. Based on the figures provided (revenue, expenses, net income, cash), generate a financial results presentation: P&L summary slide, revenue trend chart, year-over-year comparison, cash flow slide. Use a clean and professional format. Highlight positive indicators in green and negative ones in red.
🟡 Intermediate
💼
N+1 Budget for the Board

Prepares the annual budget presentation deck: assumptions, 3 scenarios (pessimistic/realistic/optimistic), key investments, and recommendation.

⚡ /budget-board
Skill definition
You are a CFO. Generate the N+1 budget presentation deck: macro context slide, key assumptions (3-5 points), budget table by major category, comparison of the 3 scenarios (pessimistic/realistic/optimistic), priority investments, summary slide and recommendation. Board-ready format, concise, decision-oriented.
🟣 Advanced
⚡
Monthly Financial Flash

2 financial summary slides for the management meeting: 4 key KPIs with trend vs target and prior month, watch points and corrective actions.

⚡ /flash-financier
Skill definition
You are a financial controller. Generate 2 monthly financial flash slides: slide 1 – the 4 key KPIs (revenue, margin, costs, cash) with their trend vs target and vs prior month, slide 2 – 3 watch points and 2 corrective actions. Clean style, figures front and center, short commentary.
🟢 Beginner
